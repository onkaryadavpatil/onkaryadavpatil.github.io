BITSIZE Publisher

BITSIZE Publisher is a small personal tool used by one person, the owner of the BITSIZE YouTube channel (short English-learning videos). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The tool does two things, both for the owner's own accounts:

  • uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel as private, scheduled uploads, and sets their thumbnails;
  • shows the publishing schedule in a Google Calendar that the tool creates in the owner's own Google account.
